# Stronghold 2 AI Overhaul v4.5 test plan

1. Generate all profiles and run `Validate-Stronghold2AIOverhaul.ps1`.
2. Install `Brutal` on a large, open map and compare construction progress with a
   vanilla game.
3. At 10, 20, and 40 minutes record food, housing, market, weapon-production, and
   military buildings.
4. Confirm each named lord retains a distinct castle and that gates and towers are
   accessible from the intended courtyard.
5. Repeat on a cramped map with `Balanced` and record incomplete walls, blocked
   workers, or unplaceable buildings.
6. Restore the backup and verify that the launcher reports the original files.
