# Stronghold 2 AI Overhaul v4.5 - Strategic Build Order

- Added building-aware construction priorities for non-campaign AI castle
  templates.
- Economy and housing are advanced first, followed by weapon production and
  military infrastructure.
- Retained the four `Balanced`, `Hard`, `Brutal`, and `Overlord` profiles and their
  compatible same-lord or same-material castle substitutions.
- Preserved castle geometry, coordinates, rotations, flags, and construction
  material.
- Renamed the double-click entry point to `Launcher.cmd`.
- Kept the PublicSafe release source-only: castle and rules profiles are generated
  locally from the user's own Stronghold 2 installation.
